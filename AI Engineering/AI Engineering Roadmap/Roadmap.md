# 📝 Our Teaching Pattern From Now On

For **every individual topic**, I'll teach in this order:

### 1. Concept

What are we learning?

### 2. Standard Definition ⭐

The definition you should remember for interviews.

### 3. Why?

Why was this concept needed?

### 4. Intuition

Simple explanation so you actually understand it.

### 5. How It Works

Step-by-step technical explanation.

### 6. Example

Simple practical example.

### 7. Important Points ⭐

Things you should remember.

### 8. Interview Questions & Answers 🎯

Important questions + **interview-ready answers**.

### 9. Common Mistakes

Things candidates commonly misunderstand.

### 10. Real-World Connection

Where you'll encounter this in actual AI systems.

### 11. Notes 📝

Clean notes that you can directly put into Notion.

### 12. Missing / Advanced Topics

We'll explicitly say:

> **Not needed now → covered later in Phase X**

So we don't accidentally skip something important.

---

# 🔥 One Important Change

We're **not going to optimize for "finishing phases quickly."**

We're optimizing for:

**Understanding + Interview Readiness + Engineering Ability.**

So if a topic is small, we'll finish it quickly.

If a topic is fundamental—like **Transformers, Attention, Embeddings, RAG, Agents, Evaluation, or AI System Design**—we'll go much deeper.

And throughout the roadmap I'll continuously distinguish between:

- 🟢 **Must know**
- 🟡 **Good to know**
- 🔵 **Advanced — later**
- 🔴 **Not required for our current goal**

That prevents you from drowning in the enormous amount of AI content available today.
