# 1.6 — Why LLMs Became Possible

This is another major conceptual topic.

We'll understand the combination of:

### 1. Data

Huge amounts of text became available.

### 2. Compute

GPUs enabled large-scale neural network training.

### 3. Algorithms

Deep learning and Transformer architectures improved dramatically.

### 4. Scale

Models became dramatically larger.

### 5. Pretraining

Models could learn general language representations from massive datasets.

### 6. Infrastructure

Distributed training and modern AI infrastructure made large models practical.

The important idea:

> **LLMs did not suddenly appear because of one invention. They emerged from the combination of better architectures, enormous datasets, massive compute, and scalable training techniques.**
