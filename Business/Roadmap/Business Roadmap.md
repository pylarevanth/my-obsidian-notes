### 🚀 AI Business Intelligence & Decision Platform

A system where a business can provide/stream its business data and ask questions such as:

- Why did revenue decrease?
- Which customer segment is most valuable?
- Which products are underperforming?
- What is causing customer churn?
- Which market segment should we target?
- What happens if we increase pricing?
- Which products should receive more investment?
- What are our biggest business risks?

The system should **investigate, analyze, reason, use tools, retrieve information, and provide evidence-backed recommendations**.

It should not merely generate text.
